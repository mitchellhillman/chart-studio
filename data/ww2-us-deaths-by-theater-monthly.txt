SOURCES: ww2-us-deaths-by-theater-monthly.csv

Metric: US Army military deaths per month, by theater (all causes, all death
types combined). US Army and Army Air Forces only. Excludes US Navy and Marine
Corps. Monthly is the finest granularity available.

Theater mapping:
  Europe                -> European Theater (ETO): NW Europe from Normandy on
  North Africa and Italy -> Mediterranean Theater (MTO): N. Africa, Sicily,
                            Salerno, Anzio, Italian campaign, southern France
  Pacific               -> consolidated Pacific theaters (excludes CBI)

Primary source:
  "Army Battle Casualties and Nonbattle Deaths in World War II, Final Report,
  7 December 1941 - 31 December 1946." US Adjutant General's Office, 1953.
  Deaths-by-month-by-theater tables ("All Army deaths in Air Corps and all
  other branches, by month and type of death").

  HyperWar (deaths section):
    https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USA/ref/Casualties/Casualties-2.html
  Report index:
    https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USA/ref/Casualties/index.html
  DTIC full PDF:
    https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/tr/pdf/ADA438106.pdf

Notes:
- The report has no monthly cross-tab of battle CASUALTIES by theater; monthly
  deaths by theater is the only true month-by-theater series it contains.
- The series begins 1941-12, the only 1941 month (Pacific 506; Europe and
  North Africa/Italy effectively 0).
- The report also lists a small separate "Africa-Middle East Theater" (rear
  area) not folded into the figures here.
